# ⚽ FIFA World Cup Dashboard (1930–2022)

## 🎯 Project Objective

This project aims to turn historical FIFA World Cup data into an interactive dashboard that makes it easy to explore and understand the tournament's evolution over nearly a century.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How has the number of goals per match changed over the years?
- Which national teams have been the most successful and consistent?
- How do host countries perform compared to other teams?
- How do attendance and stadium capacity vary across tournaments?
- What are the trends in players' positions, ages, and tournament participation?

## 📊 Data Overview

The analysis is based on 17 datasets covering 30 men's and women's tournaments, including matches, teams, players, squads, stadiums, referees, managers, standings, and attendance.

## ❓ Key Questions & KPIs

### Business Questions

**Tournaments**
- How has the average number of goals per match changed from 1930 to 2022?
- How has the number of participating teams grown over time?
- Do host countries have an advantage in winning the title?

**Teams**
- Which national teams have won the most titles and appeared in the most tournaments?
- Which teams have the best win rate and goal difference?
- How do results differ across confederations (UEFA, CONMEBOL, CAF, etc.)?

**Matches**
- What share of matches ends in a home win, away win, or draw?
- How often are matches decided by extra time or penalty shootouts?
- What were the biggest wins in World Cup history?

**Stadiums & Attendance**
- Which tournaments had the highest total and average attendance?
- Which stadiums host the most matches and have the largest capacity?
- How close is actual attendance to stadium capacity?

**Players, Managers & Referees**
- What is the age distribution of players, and how is it split by position?
- Which players have appeared in the most tournaments and matches?
- Which referees have officiated the most matches?
